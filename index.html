Privacy Policy

Royal Puzzle
Effective Date: May 2, 2026
Summary: Royal Puzzle does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The game operates entirely offline on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Royal Puzzle mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use it. By downloading, installing, or using Royal Puzzle, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Royal Puzzle mobile application and does not apply to any third-party services, websites, or platforms (including the Google Play Store) through which you may obtain the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Royal Puzzle is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App does not collect, transmit, or process any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, postal address)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID, Android ID)
  • Location data (GPS coordinates, IP-based location, network-based location)
  • Contacts, photos, files, or any other content stored on your device
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, or telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Biometric or health information
  • Financial or payment information

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

For gameplay functionality, Royal Puzzle stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Best completion times for each puzzle size (4×4 through 9×9)
  • Total number of puzzles you have solved
  • Your selected language preference (English or Arabic)

This data is stored using Android's standard local storage mechanisms and never leaves your device. We do not have access to it. You may delete this data at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset All Progress" option within the App's Settings menu, or
  • Uninstalling the App from your device

4. Internet and Network Access

Royal Puzzle does not require internet access to function. The App:

  • Does not connect to any external servers
  • Does not send or receive data over the internet
  • Does not download remote configurations or content
  • Does not request network permissions on your device

5. Third-Party Services

Royal Puzzle does not integrate with any third-party services, software development kits (SDKs), or libraries that collect user data. Specifically, the App does not use:

  • Advertising networks (no advertisements are displayed in the App)
  • Analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or others)
  • Crash reporting or error tracking services
  • Social media SDKs or integrations
  • Cloud storage or backup services
  • Push notification services

Note: The Google Play Store, through which you download the App, has its own privacy practices governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for Google's data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Royal Puzzle is suitable for users of all ages and contains no content inappropriate for children. Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below, although we do not foresee any circumstance in which this would occur given the App's offline-only design.

7. Permissions

Royal Puzzle does not request any runtime permissions on your Android device. The App does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts, calendar, or call logs
  • Location services
  • External storage, photos, or media files
  • SMS, phone, or call functionality
  • Network or internet connection

8. Data Security

Because Royal Puzzle does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no centralized data that could be subject to a breach. All gameplay data remains exclusively on your device, where security is governed by your device's operating system and your own security practices.

9. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

Since we do not collect, store, or process any personal data on our systems, there is no personal data on our side for you to access, correct, port, or request deletion of. You retain complete control over the gameplay data stored locally on your device, and you may delete it at any time as described in Section 3.

If you are a resident of a jurisdiction with specific data protection laws (such as the European Union's GDPR, California's CCPA, or Saudi Arabia's PDPL), please note that these laws govern the collection and processing of personal data. As Royal Puzzle does not collect or process personal data, these laws have limited applicability to the App's operation.
