Privacy Policy

Royal Puzzle
Effective Date: November 1, 2025
Summary: Royal Puzzle does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game works entirely offline, on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Royal Puzzle ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. By downloading or using Royal Puzzle, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Royal Puzzle is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID)
  • Location data (GPS, IP-based location, or any location information)
  • Contacts, photos, or any files from your device
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports containing personal information

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Royal Puzzle stores the following information only on your device for gameplay purposes:

  • Best completion times for each puzzle size (4×4 through 9×9)
  • Number of puzzles solved
  • Language preference (English or Arabic)

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. You can delete this information at any time using the "Reset All Progress" option in the app's Settings.

4. Internet Access

Royal Puzzle does not require internet access to function. The game works completely offline. The App does not connect to any servers, send data over the internet, or receive remote configurations.

5. Third-Party Services

Royal Puzzle does not use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Advertising networks (no ads are displayed)
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage or backup services

6. Children's Privacy

Royal Puzzle is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since the App does not collect any personal information, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

7. Permissions

Royal Puzzle does not request any sensitive permissions on your Android device. The App does not need access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other device features beyond what is necessary to display the game interface.

8. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breaches affecting your personal information through the App. All gameplay data remains on your device under your control.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

10. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any of your personal data, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, or delete from our systems. All your gameplay data is stored locally on your device, and you have full control over it through the App's Settings.
